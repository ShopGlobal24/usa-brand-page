About ShopGlobal

Two headquarters — Dubai and Mumbai. Five regions already built out underneath them.

ShopGlobal is a cross-border e-commerce company built to help global brands enter, launch and scale across the Middle East, India, Singapore, Australia and Japan — without setting up a single entity abroad themselves. Our dual headquarters in Dubai and Mumbai sit at the physical and time-zone midpoint of the GCC, India and Asia-Pacific, and together act as importer and merchant of record, manage regional compliance, and run marketplace operations directly across all five regions. Dedicated sourcing entities in New Jersey and Delaware (USA) and in the United Kingdom let us procure directly from US and European suppliers on your behalf, using partner warehouses and fulfillment centers in both countries to receive, consolidate and quality-check stock before it's freighted onward.

The ShopGlobal advantage

What actually moves the needle for a global brand, once you're listed.

Getting a global brand listed abroad is the easy part. These are the systems we run underneath it to keep your brand ranking, in stock, and protected across five regions.

01

Data-driven merchandising

A technology dashboard tracks competitor pricing and buyer behaviour per region, so listing and pricing decisions from the Gulf to Japan are backed by data, not guesswork. AI-based curation surfaces the right assortment for each market, while automated replenishment keeps stock aligned to real demand.

02

Warehousing & fulfillment

Inventory freighted into our Dubai hub can be redistributed to KSA, India, Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Australia and Japan. On the sourcing side, partner warehouses and fulfillment centers in the US and UK let us consolidate and quality-check stock close to your suppliers before it ever ships. Local warehouse infrastructure and a WMS forecast demand and keep delivery times short. Seller Flex and Vendor Flex facilities feed Amazon fulfillment directly.

03

In-house advertising

Our own team, based in Dubai, runs marketplace ad campaigns (PPC/AMS), SEO and social strategy across every region — a full 360° push for visibility, not a bolt-on agency. Every brand runs off one AI ads dashboard with direct API integrations into each marketplace account.

04

Compliance, end to end

Trademark and product registration, regulatory approvals and import documentation for your brand are handled per region before you're expected to sell a single unit.

05

Relationships that get you heard

Long-standing relationships with marketplaces and regulators across the Gulf, India and Asia-Pacific mean faster approvals, better support, and issues resolved quickly — not stuck in a generic support queue.

06

15+ years of financial integrity: scale internationally with zero settlement risk

Expanding globally requires trust, capital and speed. Since 2010, we have built an unblemished track record of financial reliability — guaranteeing 100% on-time payment settlement within agreed terms, so you can scale into new markets with complete peace of mind.

How global brands work with us

Pick the model that fits how you already ship.

ShopGlobal works as an exclusive distribution partner for select brands, and as an aggregator bringing a curated brand selection into each region. However your supply chain is set up today, there's a fulfillment model that fits, routed through Dubai.

Stock with us

Freight merchandise into our Dubai hub, or feed it directly into marketplace fulfillment centers, across every region we operate in — the UAE, KSA, India, Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Australia and Japan.

  • Shorter delivery timelines to buyers
  • WMS-backed demand forecasting
  • Built for established, repeat SKUs

Ship as you sell

Just-in-time fulfillment. Stock with us in Dubai, and we ship to each of the five regions as orders come in.

  • No upfront inventory commitment
  • Orders routed to the nearest hub
  • Good fit for testing a new region

Dropship

Keep inventory on your side and let ShopGlobal manage the marketplace front end, orders and customer experience abroad.

  • Lowest operational lift for your team
  • Fast to launch a new SKU or region
  • Flexible as demand is validated
